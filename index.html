What does the science say?

Our systematic literature review found that music therapy shows the strongest evidence for stress reduction, while general self-selected music is effective for subjective stress relief in daily life. Interestingly, classical music does not always outperform simple sounds like rippling water on physiological stress markers. The key moderating factor is listening intention: music chosen specifically for relaxation yields the best results.
